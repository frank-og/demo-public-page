Superyacht living, under one hundred feet

CONTESSA belongs to the 2024 Sunseeker 98 series, known officially as the 100 Yacht. She blends the sleek styling of a superyacht with design features rarely seen at this size, offering a seamless connection between interior and exterior living.

Her standout walk-around upper deck, forward-facing full-beam owner's suite and innovative beach club layout deliver a lifestyle that rivals far larger yachts. Built for speed, elegance and versatility, she is among the newest and most efficient options in her class, with just 215 engine hours.

"The industry-first flybridge-to-foredeck walkaround lets guests move freely between every outdoor zone on board."Sunseeker 100 Yacht — nominated and awarded for innovative layout and bold exterior design

Three decks, one continuous experience

From the flybridge to the beach club, every level is designed to be lived on.

Main deck

A penthouse-style salon bathed in light through floor-to-ceiling glazing, anchored by a sculptural floating staircase. Forward, the full-beam owner's suite with a private bow terrace.

Flybridge

An expansive open-air deck for entertaining, with sunpads, dining and a wet bar. The walk-around continues forward to dual foredeck lounges and an optional open-air cinema.

Beach club

Tender garage, rain shower, integrated BBQ and Sunseeker's X-TEND convertible sunbed, which turns the aft lounge to face the sea or the yacht at the touch of a button.

One yacht, cared for by Ocean Guardian

Ocean Guardian is a small charter and yacht-management house. We operate a single vessel — CONTESSA — because we believe one yacht, known intimately by one crew, delivers a better week than a fleet ever could.

Our team handles everything from the first enquiry to the last tender ride: itinerary design, provisioning, crew, maintenance and the quiet details in between. The same five people who welcome you aboard have been with CONTESSA since her delivery in 2024.

We are based in South Florida and winter in the Bahamas, with selected Mediterranean weeks each summer.

Guardianship, not just charter

We protect the yacht, the crew and the waters we visit. Reef-safe provisioning, no single-use plastics aboard, and dive briefings that start with conservation.
